📱 1. Telefon uchun (Android)

1. Play Market'dan Kiwi Browser yuklab oling.
2. Shu brauzer orqali Instagram'ga kiring.
3. Yuqori o'ng burchakdagi 3 nuqtani bosib, Extensions ni tanlang.
4. Cookie-Editor ni o'rnating va uni oching.
5. Ro'yxatdan sessionid degan qatorni topib, yonidagi "Copy" belgisini bosing.

💻 2. Kompyuter uchun (Chrome / Edge)

1. Brauzerda Instagram'ga kiring.
2. Klaviaturadan F12 tugmasini bosing (yoki o'ng tugma -> Inspect).
3. Application (Ilova) bo'limiga o'ting.
4. Chap menyudan Cookies -> https://www.instagram.com ni tanlang.
5. sessionid qatoridagi Value ustunidagi uzun matnni nusxalab oling.
